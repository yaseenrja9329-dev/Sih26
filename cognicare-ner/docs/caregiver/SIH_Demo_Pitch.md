# CogniCare NER — SIH 2026 Demo & Pitch

## 1. Opening

CogniCare NER is an AI-based cognitive gaming and memory assistance
platform designed to support elderly users and their caregivers.

Our focus is not only on cognitive activities, but also on helping
caregivers understand activity patterns over time.

---

## 2. The Problem

Elderly people experiencing memory and cognitive difficulties may need
regular cognitive engagement and support in their daily routines.

Caregivers may also find it difficult to continuously understand:

- Whether activities are being completed
- How the person is performing over time
- Which activities need attention
- Whether reminders are being followed
- What patterns are appearing in activity performance

CogniCare brings these signals together in one caregiver-friendly
dashboard.

---

## 3. Our Solution

CogniCare combines:

1. Cognitive games
2. Memory assistance
3. Activity reminders
4. Caregiver monitoring
5. Performance trends
6. Activity alerts
7. Patient-caregiver consent and linking

The caregiver dashboard provides a simple overview rather than
overloading the caregiver with technical information.

## 5. Important Safety Position

CogniCare is a cognitive engagement and caregiver-awareness platform.

It should NOT be presented as:

- A diagnostic system
- A dementia severity detector
- A disease progression predictor
- A replacement for a doctor

Preferred wording:

"Performance pattern noticed."

"Performance has changed compared with the person's recent activity."

Avoid:

"Patient's Alzheimer's has worsened."

"Patient has progressed to another stage."

---

## 6. What Makes CogniCare Different?

Our focus is the complete caregiver-supported workflow:

Cognitive Activities
        ↓
Activity Performance
        ↓
Performance Trends
        ↓
Caregiver Alerts
        ↓
Reminders
        ↓
Caregiver Awareness

The platform connects cognitive engagement with caregiver visibility
rather than treating the games as an isolated feature.

---

## 7. Closing

CogniCare NER aims to make cognitive engagement more structured,
caregiver-supported and accessible.

The goal is to help caregivers notice activity patterns, support
regular engagement and stay connected with the person's daily
cognitive activities.

It is designed to complement—not replace—professional medical care.
